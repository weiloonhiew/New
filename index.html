Emotion Atlas for Coaches

91 emotions, experiences & moods, mapped to the predispositions that reveal them — the 13 categories of Brené Brown's Atlas of the Heart, plus 4 foundational moods from the Ontological Coaching tradition.

Content and categorization draw on two coaching traditions: the 13-category framework in Brené Brown's Atlas of the Heart, and the "moods for action" concept from Ontological Coaching (Echeverría / Newfield), shown in its own 14th category since it treats moods as sustained dispositions rather than momentary emotions. All definitions, predisposition patterns, body cues, and coaching questions here are original material written for coaching use, not text from either source. This tool supports — and does not replace — clinical judgment or licensed mental health care.
